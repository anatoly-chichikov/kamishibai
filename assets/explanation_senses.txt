- The other lines are every other meaning named in "translation", each on its own line, none dropped and none merged with another, and besides them the meanings of the same word that the learner will soon meet in everyday talk, films or news, if there are any. Rare, technical and dated meanings that the translation does not name are left out, even when the old explanation lists them. Put the other lines in the order the learner is likely to meet them, the most common first. Five lines is the limit.
- "from" is the piece of "translation" this line stands for, copied character for character, or "" when the meaning is not named in the translation. The first line's "from" is the piece that names the meaning of the card's sentence.
