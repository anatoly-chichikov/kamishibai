SCENE-FIDELITY POLICY

Compare the actual pixels against the compact scene reference. Never infer that a required element is visible merely because the reference says it exists. Inspect every panel and ground each clear omission in scene_fidelity_evidence with the required element, what the pixels actually show instead, a coarse image location, and one kind:
- MISSING_REQUIRED_SUBJECT: a distinct person, animal, or acting object required for a panel's semantic job, visible anchor, literal event, or relation is absent from the rendered image;
- MISSING_REQUIRED_RELATION: the required participants may be present, but the interaction, opposition, transfer, comparison, spatial relation, or emotional relation needed to express the semantic job is absent;
- MISSING_LITERAL_ANCHOR: a concrete required object, action, condition, or event named by the literal event or literal anchor is absent;
- BROKEN_SUBJECT_CONTINUITY: the same nonempty subject id required across panels is visibly rendered as a different person, animal, or acting object, including substitution of another declared subject; or distinct required subject ids are collapsed or swapped so the required actions or roles attach to the wrong participant.

Use scene_fidelity_decision=REJECT when at least one clear omission or continuity failure is grounded, otherwise ALLOW with an empty scene_fidelity_evidence array. The evidence kinds are authoritative even when the proposed decision contradicts them. A required subject must be visibly distinguishable in the pixels; a crowd silhouette or another subject cannot silently substitute for it when its role is necessary to the relation. Subject ids in the reference are identity anchors across panels, not visible text and never proof that the rendered figures match. For BROKEN_SUBJECT_CONTINUITY, ground both affected panels and the stable visible figure cues or actions that prove one role was repeated while another required subject disappeared, collapsed, or swapped. Do not reject camera angle, framing, pose, ordinary clothing variation, lighting, hair rendering, facial angle, or minor drawing inconsistency alone when stable figure cues and the required identity remain clear. Intended changes in action, emotion, or narrative beat by the same identifiable subject are allowed. Do not turn scene-fidelity failures into semantic answer-leakage evidence or literal-writing evidence.

An unmarked, blank, empty, or featureless calendar, document, screen, or board is pixel evidence only of a blank carrier. Never infer a date, deadline, overdue or missed state, status, data, progress, result, or entries from that blank carrier or from the untrusted reference claiming that it shows them. If a required information-bearing state or contrast lacks another independent pixel-grounded cue, ground MISSING_LITERAL_ANCHOR or MISSING_REQUIRED_RELATION with what the pixels actually omit. A blank carrier used only as an incidental object or background remains allowed when no semantic requirement depends on it carrying information.

LITERAL-WRITING POLICY

Inspect the entire illustration for localized writing-like content, whether or not it reveals the answer. Ground every observation in literal_evidence with a short visual description, a coarse location, and one kind:
- WRITING: any visible letter, word, label, caption, logo text, or writing in any language;
- NUMERAL: any visible digit or written number;
- MATHEMATICAL_NOTATION: any formula, equation, operator string, plotted formula label, or mathematical notation;
- TECHNICAL_DIAGRAM: any blueprint, floor plan, drafting or engineering plan, circuit or system schematic, or technical drawing that encodes a layout or system with conventional lines, grids, or symbols;
- PSEUDO_WRITING: organized rows, clusters, or strokes that visibly imitate handwriting or printed script even when no exact transcription is possible;
- DECORATIVE_GLYPH_STRING: repeated ornamental, cipher-like, rune-like, or invented glyphs arranged like text;
- INTERFACE_MARK: a keyboard legend, button label, state label, chart label, or text-like interface mark;
- SYMBOL_OR_EMBLEM: a deliberate applied graphic used as a logo, badge, insignia, transit mark, or brand-like icon, or an isolated glyph intentionally displayed within or on a badge, medallion, patch, placard, or emblem position;
- AMBIGUOUS_MARK: an isolated mark that cannot be distinguished from hatching, texture, motion lines, architecture, or an object contour.

WRITING, NUMERAL, MATHEMATICAL_NOTATION, TECHNICAL_DIAGRAM, PSEUDO_WRITING, DECORATIVE_GLYPH_STRING, INTERFACE_MARK, and SYMBOL_OR_EMBLEM are prohibited and require literal_writing_present=true. AMBIGUOUS_MARK is allowed and does not make literal_writing_present true. Hatching, texture, speed lines, shading, folds, object contours, and pictorial silhouettes are not evidence unless they form localized writing-like structure. Do not infer letters from the scene narrative. Never mention suspected formulas, technical diagrams, numerals, pseudo-writing, decorative glyph strings, symbols, or emblems only in reason: ground each one in literal_evidence. Use an empty literal_evidence array and literal_writing_present=false only after checking the entire image and finding none.

Any populated ledger, receipt, form, ruled table, or notebook containing repeated short horizontal strokes, ticks, stamps, circled tallies, row entries, or line-like record marks must be PSEUDO_WRITING even when no character is readable. The printed rules or blank grid alone are not writing; the localized marks filling its rows or cells are. Never dismiss a populated record surface as table structure, hatching, texture, or an empty document.

A technical plan must be TECHNICAL_DIAGRAM even when it contains no readable character. Do not classify an ordinary building or object drawing, a genuinely blank grid, natural contours, hatching, or texture as a technical diagram; the prohibited kind requires a structured drawing that visibly encodes a layout or system through conventional plan or schematic marks.

A deliberate logo, badge, insignia, transit mark, or brand-like icon is SYMBOL_OR_EMBLEM even when it cannot be recognized or read. An isolated glyph enclosed or placed as an emblem is also SYMBOL_OR_EMBLEM when it is a distinct inner graphic independent of its physical housing. Enclosure or centering alone is insufficient: ordinary object contours, mechanical hardware, lights, or genuinely blank geometric panels are not evidence, nor are blank plates or displays, headlights, taillights, lamps, reflectors, bolts, screws, handles, latches, hinges, vents, grilles, couplers, wipers, or door and window seams. If no distinct applied inner graphic can be grounded, classify the mark as AMBIGUOUS_MARK.

PAGE-FRAME POLICY

Classify how the page presents its panel-frame structure into page_frame:
- FRAMED: the page reads as a manga page with clean visible frame structure — panel border lines, white gutters between panels, or a white page margin;
- BLEED: frame structure is clearly present somewhere, but at least one panel extends over the page edge or the outer margin is partly covered by artwork; this is an accepted stylistic device, never a defect;
- BREAKOUT: a subject or panel content deliberately crosses a panel border into a neighboring panel, or one composition intentionally spans several panel areas — a figure stepping out of its frame, a splash spreading across half the page — while the remaining frame lines stay clean and deliberate; this is an accepted stylistic device, never a defect;
- TORN: the frame lines themselves carry a generation artifact — smeared, broken, doubled, or garbage-filled border segments that read as rendering damage rather than an intentional device;
- BORDERLESS: nowhere in the entire image is any panel border line, white gutter, or page margin visible — the picture is one single unframed full-page image with no frame structure at all.

Precedence: choose BORDERLESS only under that strict everywhere-absent condition; otherwise choose TORN whenever any border segment reads as damaged, even when other panels also bleed or break out; otherwise choose BREAKOUT or BLEED for the intact stylistic devices; otherwise FRAMED. Never classify a clean deliberate crossing as TORN — TORN requires visible damage on the frame lines themselves. This classification is independent of every other decision and never influences them.

Pseudo-writing and decorative glyph strings, together with symbols, emblems, and technical diagrams, have no semantic reading and must never become semantic evidence merely because the depicted activity suggests the hidden answer.

Unrelated labels, isolated numerals, one non-focus word from the target sentence, pseudo-writing, technical diagrams, decorative marks, and ambiguous glyphs do not count as answer leakage. Writing that merely repeats information already present in the SHOWN fields is also semantically allowed. This semantic ALLOW never overrides literal_writing_present or prohibited literal_evidence.
